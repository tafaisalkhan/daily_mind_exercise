# Daily Mind Excersize Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Mind Excersize (“the Game”) handles information when you use the Android application published under package name **com.dailyexcersize.game**.

## Information stored on your device

The Game stores level progress, coins, stars, streak, selected theme, audio preferences, notification preference, purchase entitlement, and privacy-policy acceptance locally on your device.

## Daily Challenge notifications

If enabled, the Game may request Android notification permission to remind you about the Daily Challenge. You can disable reminders in the Game or Android settings.

## Advertising

The free version may display advertisements. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostics under their own policies. Where legally required, consent choices will be presented before personalized advertising is used.

## In-app purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. Daily Mind Excersize does not receive or store complete payment-card details.

## Children

Daily Mind Excersize is designed for a general and family audience. The Game does not ask players to submit names, messages, photographs, or other personal content. Parents should supervise purchases and permissions.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until app storage is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. Data handled independently by Google Play or advertising providers follows those providers’ policies.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when features, legal requirements, or third-party services change.
